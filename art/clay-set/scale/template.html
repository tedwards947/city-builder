One metre for the whole city

The highway's four lanes fit in one tile, so a tile is about 17 m. Everything below is drawn at game scale against that. The houses, shops and factories are the real sprites. The mid-rise and the tower are code-drawn massing blocks, here only to judge size; they are not the final art.

The metre

5.9 px/macross, in a 128 px sprite (plot is 101 px)

0.12 tileone floor on screen, in the game

The floor height comes from the art you said looks right (level 1–2 houses, the current level 3 towers' window rows). Measured, not guessed: 11 px per floor at 128 px across all of them.

Questions for this round

  1. Tower height. True, 75% or 50%? And how many floors for the tallest 2×2 tower? A true-height 20-floor tower covers about 2½ tiles behind it, so hover-to-hide matters more the taller we go.
  2. 1×1 level 3 mid-rise. Is 4 floors on a block about 12 m square the right look for the top of a 1×1 lot? (Shops 2–4 floors, industry a bigger works, as you said.) This replaces today's thin 6–8 floor towers, which are only about 5 m wide.
  3. Trucks and trains. Trucks at real length (9 m) take two traffic slots instead of one, and train cars at 20 m make a 3-car train 3½ tiles long. Both are code changes. Go ahead?
  4. Ships. Real container ships would be 8+ tiles long. Keep the small 2¼-tile ships, go to about 4 tiles, or leave ships for later?
