One metre for the whole city

The highway's four lanes fit in one tile, so a tile is about 17 m. Everything below is drawn at game scale against that. The houses, shops and factories are the real sprites. The mid-rise and the tower are code-drawn massing blocks, here only to judge size; they are not the final art.

The metre
17 mone tile (4 lanes × 3.1 m + verges)
5.9 px/macross, in a 128 px sprite (plot is 101 px)
11 pxone 3 m floor, upward, in a 128 px sprite
0.12 tileone floor on screen, in the game
4.5 ma car: about ¼ tile, never ⅓

The floor height comes from the art you said looks right (level 1–2 houses, the current level 3 towers' window rows). Measured, not guessed: 11 px per floor at 128 px across all of them.
