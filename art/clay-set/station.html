Station resize workshop

The new sizes, as agreed with the engine session: a straight station is 3 tiles in a row (1 deep), a curved one is 2×2 with the house in the corner tile, and a subway platform is 3 tiles. No track may run right beside a platform, and roads can join from the long sides, so everything has to fit inside the station's own tiles: nothing hangs over the road next door. The dashed outline is the station's footprint.

These are layouts drawn in code, with no paid images. Once you've picked a layout, the Victorian house and canopies get painted to fit it (after your OK for paid images).
