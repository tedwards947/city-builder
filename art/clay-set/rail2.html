Rail workshop 2

Your round 1 picks are built in: T1 classic track, S1 Victorian stations, E2 steel elevated deck, U1 blueprint underground view, J1 plain junctions and the C1–C3 liveries. New this round: two tracks on every rail tile, with trains running on the right-hand track, and the trains drawn top-down like the road cars, in code, so they turn smoothly round curves. Each train fades in, pulls in to a station, waits, leaves and fades out. Trains not stopping at a station just fade in and out.

This is still one line in the game, not two: the engine keeps one track per tile, and the second track is drawn only. The brutalist station (S2) is set aside for a later "large train station".
