Clay roads and traffic ideas

All drawn by code. Three sections: the road classes, junction ideas (J1–J4), and ways to show traffic without free-roaming cars (T1–T6, animated). Tell me letters, mixes, or what is missing.

1. Road classes

Highway across the top (dark, barriers, no pavement), an avenue down the left and across the lower half (fills the tile, four lanes, planted median), streets everywhere else (narrow, two lanes, pavement, grass verge). Bridges where they cross the river. Shown with junction idea J2 and no traffic.

2. Junction ideas

The same corner of town four ways: avenue crossing avenue on the left, avenue narrowing to a street, a street crossroads and a dead end on the right.

3. Ways to show traffic

Same map, same traffic in each: the lower avenue is busy, the middle street between the avenue and the river is jammed (over capacity), the highway is moderate, side streets are quiet. None of these has cars that drive around the map; each road tile shows its own load.
