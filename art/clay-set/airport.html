Airport workshop

One airport, 12×5 tiles: a single runway running left to right (it is runway 9, so planes land left→right; the far end reads 27 as on a real runway), one terminal with three jet bridges, a tower and a hangar. Every scene loops through about a minute: a plane lands, rolls out, leaves the runway, pulls up to the third jet bridge, gets bags and fuel, is pushed back by a tug, taxis out and takes off. The plane is one flat painted picture that never turns: it parks side-on beside the terminal and the bridge reaches its far-side door.

New art: 8 paid images, as agreed (5 terminal sheets, planes, ground vehicles, 1 redo that came back unchanged). The houses, shop, office tower and large power plant beside each airport are the game's own sprites, for size. Mark Yes or No, then Copy feedback and paste it to Claude. You can say yes to more than one in a section.

How it might work in the game (imagined, not built): like the ships, nothing is simulated. What's happening at the airport comes from the clock and the airport's flights a day, so it costs nothing off screen and needs no saving: one arrival-and-departure cycle at a time, planes fading in out over the map's west edge on approach and fading out after take-off.
