Jumping: the whole toast is clickable, with a chevron (J1); on arrival the asking area is outlined and blinks (J4). Progress: a progress line and bar in the badge hover (P1), and hovering a badge outlines its area the way J4 does (the owner's change to P2: the area, not each home). Placement: a warning in the price tag when a service building would not touch a road; it can still be placed (W1).

Numbers and places are made up to fill the pictures. The Yes / No controls still work if anything needs revisiting.
