Rail workshop

Rail as agreed in RAIL_DESIGN.md: one track network on three levels (ground, elevated, subway). Track, pillars, tunnels and junctions are drawn in code, and this page shows how they could look, at the game's scale, next to the game's own houses and shops. The stations, subway entrance, ramps and train cars will be paintings. Until those are made, they show here as dashed boxes and plain code trains.

Mark Yes or No, then Copy feedback and paste it to Claude. You can say yes to more than one in a section.

T. Ground track

A main line through a straight station, a branch off a 3-way junction, a 4-way crossing and two level crossings where roads cross the track. At a level crossing the barriers come down and cars wait while a train passes.

E. Elevated track

The line climbs a ramp (drawn in code here; the real ramp will be a painting), runs on pillars over a road, and splits at a 3-way junction with a branch running north over another road. Its shadow falls down and to the right, like the buildings' shadows.

U. Subway and the underground view

Each scene switches between the surface and the underground view every few seconds. The subway line comes out of a ground↔subway portal on the left, has a 4-tile platform under the street (its 1×1 entrance is on the surface) and a branch heading north. The subway train shows only in the underground view. On the ground stretch you see it all the time.

J. Junctions up close

Junctions are rail's weak point: a 3-way junction has a penalty and a 4-way junction a much bigger one. These close-ups ask whether the map should show that.
