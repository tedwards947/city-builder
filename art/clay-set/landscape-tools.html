Landscape tools mocks

Mocks for the new Landscape tools (LANDSCAPING_DESIGN.md): plant trees of a chosen species with a brush, erase trees, and add or remove water. Beaches follow the water by themselves, using today's sand art. The toolbars below copy the game's own styles. The map scenes are drawn by code with the real clay trees, so no paid images. All prices are placeholders until the lab sets them.
